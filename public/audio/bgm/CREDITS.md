## Changes made for this app

The original Ogg Vorbis tracks were transcoded to stereo, 44.1 kHz MP3 using FFmpeg 7.1 / libmp3lame (`-q:a 4`) for browser and mobile compatibility. A uniform gain of -3 dB was applied to leave headroom after lossy encoding. The musical content and duration were retained, and filenames and metadata were set as listed above. There are no vocals, added sound effects, cuts, or fades introduced by this app.

The five MP3 files total approximately 5.51 MB. The original Ogg files are designed as loops; the exact continuity of repeated MP3 playback depends on the browser's handling of encoder delay and its playback implementation.
